# Rigid Bodies 1.2.0

This release adds Chasing light, a special-relativity experiment that shows where Newtonian
mechanics stops applying, and fixes Measure navigation, lab-setting edits during a run, value
menus and number display throughout the application.

## Chasing light

- Added **Chasing light** in a new Special relativity collection. A 1 kg probe carrying its own
  clock races a light pulse along a track ten light-nanoseconds long (about 3 m), with a new pulse
  on every lap. Time runs a billion times slower than real time: at 1×, one second on screen is
  one nanosecond in the lab, and the status line and Present strip show **Lab time**.
- Set **Probe speed** from rest to 0.9999999 c by typing values such as `0.99 c`, `99.9 %` or
  `150000 km/s`, by dragging its slider, which moves in equal boosts, or with preset chips from
  Rest to 0.99999 c. Up and Down nudge the speed, ten presses adding a nine near c; Shift+Up and
  Shift+Down step through the presets and on to 0.9999999 c. Clicking the probe or pressing **W**
  opens Probe speed in the Inspector.
- Speeds above 0.9999999 c, including c itself, are refused with an explanation. Readings never
  round up to c: v/c keeps its nines, the speed is cut towards rest and **Below c by** is rounded
  up. A moving probe needs at least 0.000000000001 c.
- The stage shows a lab clock and a clock riding the probe, how far the probe's clock has fallen
  behind, and the light's lead and winning margin on each lap. A band across the top reads rest
  mass, v, v/c, γ, kinetic energy, momentum and a **How close to c** gauge that fills one cell per
  nine and is never full. The smallest stages draw smaller clock faces and leave out the race
  result.

## Measuring relativity

- In Chasing light, Measure offers Relativity, Graph and Runs. **Relativity** plots kinetic
  energy, momentum, γ or the probe's clock rate against speed, with Newton's prediction dashed
  beside it and the current speed marked. **Up to c** draws c as a limit and marks values beyond
  the chart at its edge; **Near c** uses a logarithmic view whose axis adds a nine with each step.
- **At this speed** lists v/c, v, how far below c, γ, kinetic energy and momentum beside Newton's
  ½mv² and mv, rest mass and rest energy mc², both clocks, their difference t − τ and the clock
  rate 1/γ.
- **Graph** plots the lab clock, probe clock, t − τ and γ over lab time in 10, 30 or 60 ns
  windows, with the previous run, a comparison run and markers where the speed changed. **Runs**
  can pin those values at the end, maximum, minimum or a lab time from 0 to 60 ns. A clock or γ
  reading's **Plot over time** and **Add to runs table…** send it straight to either tab.

## Editing, saving and teaching

- Speed changes can be undone and appear under Changes with their own revert. Undo, redo, revert
  and dragging change the clocks' rate but never rewind them, and Escape cancels a drag.
  **Back to start** zeroes the clocks at the current speed; **Restore original** also restores
  the speed.
- Saved setups store the probe's speed and rest mass. A speed edit marks the setup unsaved, but
  running does not, and a saved setup reopens with both clocks at zero.
- The Guide offers a prediction, tasks and **Show me** links to the speed controls and the
  Relativity plot. Present mode reframes the stage around the band and track and keeps the speed
  keys available under its lock.
- Controls follow the experiment: the Inspector shows only World with the probe's settings, and
  Add, Draw, Import shape, Throw, Pull and **Play until next impact** are unavailable with a
  reason. Command search and the keyboard reference list only what applies, and search recognises
  terms such as "proper time" and "Lorentz".

## Other fixes

- **Show me**, **Plot over time** and impact clicks now bring the matching Measure tab forward,
  such as Graph or Collisions, instead of opening Measure on the last tab used.
- Measure tabs and choice lists, including the Graph's scope and quantity lists, rebuild when their
  options change, for example after adding objects or opening another experiment.
- Changing a lab setting such as the integrator, time step or solver during a run, or reverting
  lab settings, no longer moves the starting state to that moment. **Back to start** returns to
  the real start, and run progress no longer appears as setup changes.
- **Hide previous run** in the Graph's context menu now hides the previous run. Value and graph
  menus open on the first right-click, at the pointer, and a reading's **Add to runs table…** and
  **Revert to original** are unavailable, with a reason, where they cannot act.
- Command search now focuses checklists such as Graph **Quantities**. Slider and drag values stay
  within a setting's limits after rounding, and a pinned value cannot be added twice.

## Numbers and text

- The bundled Inter faces gain a thin space, so grouped numbers such as 12 400 and 299 792 458
  keep their gaps in the interface and no longer show "?" on the stage. Scientific notation
  carries rounding into the exponent: 9.9996 × 10⁻⁴ reads 1.00 × 10⁻³, not 10.00 × 10⁻⁴.
- Stage label plates use the font's real widths for superscripts, µ, ω, → and the thin space, so
  they fit their text; some plates in other experiments change width slightly.
- Plot read-outs keep each value with its unit, and plot labels no longer draw with the wrong
  glyphs when new characters first appear. Disabled segmented controls appear greyed and explain
  why they are unavailable.

## Content format

- Scenario documents may list `special_relativity` in `required_features`. They must then include
  a `relativity` object with `rest_mass_kg` from 0.000001 to 1,000,000 and `speed_fraction_c` of 0
  or from 0.000000000001 to 0.9999999, or they are rejected with a message naming the field. Any
  other required feature is still rejected.
- Rigid Bodies 1.0.1 and earlier reject documents that require the feature, so saved Chasing light
  setups need 1.2.0 or later. A Guide control that belongs to the other kind of experiment says it
  does not apply.

## Maintenance and distribution

- Updated shared product metadata and About snapshots to 1.2.0.
