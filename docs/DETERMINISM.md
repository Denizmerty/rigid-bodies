# Determinism and generated physics properties

The validation suites check that the same build repeats every recorded physical value exactly,
including with one, two or four collision workers. Across supported platforms, a portable subset
must match exactly, and general mechanics must stay within specified numerical tolerances.
This does not promise that arbitrary chaotic scenes or custom components remain bit-identical
between different standard math libraries indefinitely.

## Reproducible trace

Build with `RIGIDBODIES_BUILD_TESTS=ON`. The dependency-free
`rigid_bodies_determinism_probe` writes the versioned JSON trace:

```text
rigid_bodies_determinism_probe --output trace.json --workers 4 --verify
python scripts/compare_determinism.py tests/fixtures/determinism/reference-v1.json trace.json
```

`--verify` runs the workload again with the requested worker count, then with one, two, and four
workers. Every run's canonical serialized simulation trace must match exactly. Worker evidence
confirms that both broad and narrow phases actually use the requested workers. Every workload
also restores its halfway snapshot and requires the remaining records to match exactly, including
the storage mutation after that checkpoint. There are no wall-clock values in simulation records.

The five revision-1 workloads contain 220 complete steps:

| Workload | Steps | Step size | Contract and coverage |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `dyadic_elastic_impacts` | 72 | 1/128 s | Exact values: four isolated equal-mass elastic circle pairs, no rotation or gravity. |
| `dyadic_storage_replay` | 32 | 1/128 s | Exact values: eight translating bodies, two removals and slot generation reuses, checkpoint replay. |
| `contacts_and_sensors` | 56 | 1/120 s | Bounded values: eight lanes of continuous circle impacts and sensor crossings. |
| `joints_and_forces` | 48 | 1/120 s | Bounded values: three constrained pendulums with gravity and damping. |
| `continuous_collision` | 12 | 1/60 s | Bounded values: six fast small circles crossing thin walls in one unconstrained step. |

Every step records body keys, slot generations, awake/type flags, collider counts, canonical broad
pairs, manifold identities and 64-bit feature keys, sensor/speculative flags, begin/end events,
constraint states, island membership and discrete CCD/limit counters. Body placement, velocity,
mass, energy, retained loads, contact normals/witnesses/impulses and joint reactions are retained
as numbers. Feature IDs are strings so JSON's floating-point representation cannot lose their bits.

The exact subset uses dyadic initial positions, velocities and time steps, unit mass overrides,
zero gravity, no rotation, and no iterative position correction or transcendental force profiles.
Cross-platform comparison requires exact equality of every numeric value in these two workloads;
even one ULP of drift fails. This is numeric identity, with positive and negative zero equivalent.

For the three general workloads, only values under a record's `values` field admit:

```text
abs(actual - reference) <= 2e-9 + 2e-8 * max(abs(actual), abs(reference))
```

Numbers are in the named SI units. The absolute term handles values near zero; the relative term
scales with the measured quantity. These tolerances apply to the listed workloads and durations. Every discrete field, event, ordering, number of
records and object field remains exact. Compiler and platform metadata is informative and excluded
from physical comparisons. The build disallows floating-point contraction for reproducible
operation ordering; fast-math builds are outside this contract.

The comparator rejects duplicate keys, non-finite numbers (including overflowed JSON literals),
missing scenarios or steps, altered contracts, reordered records, and missing or extra fields. Its
21 adversarial unit tests include an exact-subset one-ULP mutation, general numeric changes on both
sides of the tolerance, altered body generations/events, booleans where integers are required, and
invalid input. Run them with `python scripts/test_compare_determinism.py`.

## Portable reference maintenance

`tests/fixtures/determinism/reference-v1.json` is a checked-in physical reference, generated by the
probe after its same-build and worker verification passes. Its metadata identifies the producer.
Windows CI compares its trace with this reference after verifying same-build replay and worker
counts. The comparator also accepts traces from other compilers and platforms for manual checks.
Linux and macOS CI are currently disabled. When reporting validation, list the local and hosted
jobs that actually ran, along with their compiler and platform.

Changing a fixture requires investigating the first differing record, explaining the physics or
workload change, rerunning the property and analytical suites, and inspecting traces from the
available independent compilers. Do not regenerate it merely to make a failure pass. Intentional
workload/schema changes require a new revision, updated comparator expectations and documented
migration; machine/compiler metadata alone can change without a workload revision.

## Generated physical properties

`property_validation_tests.cpp` uses an explicit SplitMix64 generator and a fixed conversion to
53-bit uniform real values, avoiding standard-library distribution differences. Failures report
the seed and generated case index. Its five tests exercise 752 generated cases:

| Property | Cases | Conditions that make the invariant valid |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Impact momentum and energy | 256 | Two finite dynamic bodies, one shared impulse point, restitution in [0,1], no external loads, position correction or damping. |
| Frictional dissipation | 256 | Isolated pairs with restitution zero and passive Coulomb, rolling and spinning resistance. |
| Free-flight conservation | 96 | Four separated bodies, zero gravity, disabled collisions and sleep, no damping or motion clamps. |
| Damping is passive | 96 | One isolated body, no gravity or potential-energy source; every step checks kinetic energy, including large damping factors. |
| Finite state under stress and mutation | 48 | Eight bodies over 24 steps, extreme finite velocities/loads, varied masses, collisions, removal/reuse and one/four workers. |

The momentum checks include both spin and orbital angular momentum about the world origin.
Energy tests isolate passive processes: total kinetic energy need not decrease when
gravity, elastic springs, moving supports or motors can supply it. Conservation tests reject
motion-limit intervention rather than treating a clamp as physical conservation. Stress tests
instead exercise those explicit limits and require every surviving body's placement, motion,
inverse mass/inertia and energy, plus aggregate energy, to remain finite.
